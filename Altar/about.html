About Hot / Humid

Hot / Humid is a moodboard of silhouettes shaped by tropical heat: asymmetry, texture, and clothes that cling and slip at the same time.

This project spotlights the ways climate, culture, and nightlife cross-pollinate in contemporary Vietnamese and Southeast Asian style, from slinky club looks to everyday streetwear.

Click through each look to explore different “vibes” — from dark, draped minimalism to romantic ruffles and cut-outs.
